Program
    { moduleComment = Just " Cell-reuse benchmark — accumulator-based list reverse, repeated.

         The hot loop is the Lean-4 canonical cell-reuse pattern:

           revInto acc lst = case lst of
             INil -> acc
             ICons h t -> revInto (ICons h acc) t

         At every recursive step the matched-out 'ICons h t' cell is
         linear (its only use is the case scrutinee), and the arm body
         constructs a 'ICons h acc' cell of the same arity in tail
         position. 'Awsum.Reuse' detects this shape and rewrites the
         inner 'CCon 20 [h, acc]' to 'CReuse "lst" 20 [h, acc]' — in-
         place stores on the existing cell rather than '__alloc' /
         '__free' on every iter. Visible via 'awsum core'.

         On LLVM/WASM the rewrite eliminates the freelist churn: one
         '__alloc' pop + one '__free' push per cell becomes one tail-
         field write. On JVM/CLR/JS the codegen still emits a fresh
         cell allocation — managed GCs promote it to stack via escape
         analysis once the hot loop is alloc-free.

         Sizing: 'repeat 100000 1' builds a 100 000-element list of 1s;
         'revN 1000' reverses it 1 000 times. Cumulative cell traffic
         = 1e5 × 1e3 = 1e8 reuse rewrites. 'sumList' walks the final
         reversed list to force the result through addInt32 (so the
         optimiser cannot elide the recursion). "
